about me.

about me.

I'm a student at Brigham Young University in Provo, Utah, pursuing my passion for media and visual storytelling. Originally from Arizona, I grew up fascinated by the power of cameras to capture emotion, detail, and perspective. What started as a hobby quickly turned into a creative outlet that I couldn't let go of. Over time, I realized my passion wasn't just for filming — it was for storytelling, and the way video can connect people and ideas. Today, I combine both of those loves in every project I take on, whether it's capturing a wedding, building a brand video, or creating something personal. I’m driven by the belief that behind every great video is a story worth telling.

weddings.

There’s something powerful about capturing a day that means everything to two people. Weddings are filled with raw emotion, meaningful moments, and beautiful visuals that come together to tell a story that’s deeply personal. I love being able to preserve those memories in a way that feels cinematic, timeless, and true to the couple!

commercial.

I love helping brands tell their story in a way that’s clear, creative, and visually compelling. Whether it's a local business or a growing startup, I enjoy turning ideas into polished visuals that connect with real people. Video brings your message to life — and I’m all about making it memorable.

social media.

Social media is where attention lives — and I love creating short-form content that stops the scroll. Whether it’s a behind-the-scenes clip, a bold brand teaser, or a cinematic reel, I craft videos that are fast, intentional, and made to connect. Every second counts, and I make those seconds matter!
